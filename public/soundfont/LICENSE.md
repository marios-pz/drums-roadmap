# Drum sounds

The files in `percussion-mp3/` are the General MIDI percussion samples (MIDI notes 35 to 81) from the
**Fluid R3 GM** SoundFont by **Frank Wen**, as rendered to MP3 by the
[midi-js-soundfonts](https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts) project. They are the default
sounds of [abcjs](https://github.com/paulrosen/abcjs) and are copied here so the site doesn't depend
on another site being online.
